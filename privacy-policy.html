Dumpling Run – Privacy Policy

Developer: TdsGames · Effective date: November 13, 2026

In short: Dumpling Run is a family game for all ages. It has no accounts, no chat and no sign-in, and we don't collect personal information. The first time it starts, the game asks the player's age; the answer stays on the device. Players under 18 get child-safe ads that are never personalized and never use the device's advertising ID, and buying coins requires a grown-up check. Coin purchases are handled by Google Play.

1. Who we are

Dumpling Run ("the game") is made by TdsGames. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at tds1oh7@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Age question. When the game first starts, it asks "How old are you?". It only keeps which group the answer falls in (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over), on the device, and uses it to choose the ad settings described below. The age is never sent to us or to Google.

The game saves your progress on your own device: coins, unlocked dumplings, hats, faces and sparkle effects, best scores, and settings such as sound and game mode. This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. If you use Android's device backup, Android may include this game data in your Google backup and restore it on a new device.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner ad and optional video ads that a player can choose to watch (for example, to revive or to double the coins from a run). These buttons are labelled as ads. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, which is certified for Google Play's Families program. Settings depend on the player's age answer:

PlayerAd settings
Under 13Every ad request is marked child-directed (US COPPA) and under the age of consent (EU/UK law). Ads are never personalized, are limited to content rated G, and the Android advertising ID is not sent.
13 to 17Same protections as under 13: no personalized ads and no advertising ID. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.
18 and overRegular ads, limited to content suitable for teens. Google may use the Android advertising ID to personalize ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks first; you can change your choice any time with the shield (🛡️) button on the game's main menu. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Settings › Google › Ads).

To deliver ads, limit how often they're shown and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK processes this technical information, which is encrypted in transit:

DataExamples
IP addressUsed to estimate general location (for example, country or city)
Product interactionsApp launches, ad views and taps
Diagnostic informationApp and ad performance, such as load times
Device identifiersApp set ID for everyone; the advertising ID only for players 18 and over

Learn more in Google's guide to ads in apps for children, How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

For players under 18, before a coin pack can be bought, the game shows a grown-up check (numbers written as words that must be typed in). Google Play then asks for the account holder's authentication according to the account's purchase settings; parents can require approval for every purchase in the Google Play app (Settings › Authentication) or with Google Family Link. Coin packs are sold through Google Play, and Google processes the payment; we never see or store your payment card or billing details. The game only receives confirmation from Google Play of which coin pack was bought, so it can add the coins. Google's handling of purchase data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

5. Other services

The app doesn't use any other analytics, tracking or social services, and it doesn't load fonts or other content from the internet.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't require an account, a name, an email address or a phone number.
  • We don't access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We don't sell personal information.

7. Children and parents

Dumpling Run is made for players of all ages, including children, and takes part in Google Play's Families program. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children: the game has no accounts, no chat, no way to share photos or text, and doesn't ask for a name, age or email. The game has no links to websites or other apps. Parents with any question, or who want help with their child's data, can contact us at tds1oh7@gmail.com.

8. Your rights and deleting data

Because we don't hold personal information about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side. To delete your game data, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. To manage data held by Google, use your Google Account's data and privacy settings. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete data, and to object to processing. Contact us and we'll help you exercise them.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
